In 1968, a high jumper named Dick Fosbury did something everyone thought was crazy.

Every other athlete ran at the bar and jumped over it facing forward.
Fosbury sprinted in on a curve, twisted in the air, and went over the bar backwards, head first.

The crowd laughed. Then he cleared 2.24 meters and won Olympic gold.

Today almost every high jumper in the world uses his move. It's called the Fosbury Flop.
