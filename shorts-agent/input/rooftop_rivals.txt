Two rivals face each other on a rooftop at night.

The first one runs in and throws a punch. The other blocks it.
He answers with a kick. The first rival leans back and dodges.
Then a fast punch lands and knocks him back a step.

He shakes it off, backflips away, and comes back with one huge kick.
The first rival goes flying and crashes to the ground.

Slowly, he gets back up. The fight isn't over.
