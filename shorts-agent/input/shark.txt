Sharks are older than trees.

Two friends are walking through a forest. One says, "These trees must be ancient."
The other one laughs: "Sharks are older."

The first sharks were swimming in the ocean about 450 million years ago.
The first trees didn't appear until around 385 million years ago.

So sharks were already around for tens of millions of years before there was a single forest on Earth.

Next time you're at the beach, remember: that shark is from an older family than every tree you've ever seen.
